## Pre-output measurement definitions

Use one monotonic clock and existing request/phase deadlines. The baseline row interval starts immediately before constructing its baseline request and stops after the complete response is bound to that request, usage/finish status is checked, and the unchanged output-integrity verdict is available. The candidate interval starts immediately before per-row bank validation, literal selection and rendering; it ends after the same response/usage/output checks. Its two measured token-count calls for a surface-eligible row, receipt validation, token-budget decision, request serialization and any synchronous helper communication are inside that interval. An ineligible row has no measured token-count calls but still includes its actual bank/selection/render work. Do not subtract warm-process costs or move necessary work into the untimed preflight.

The helper lookup/render sample is the nested per-candidate-row interval from just before bank validation/selection to completed candidate-plan construction, before network token counting. There are exactly87 samples on a complete run, including abstentions; use nearest-rank P95 over all of them. Include the first measured sample in that population and report it separately. Label it cold only if that process and bank actually were not warmed by preflight; otherwise report genuine startup/cold cost separately without adding another helper sample or benchmark call. Helper RSS is the peak resident memory of the actual interpreter/process that owns this policy work, including its imports and bank, measured using the existing process-scoped guard. It cannot be replaced by a module-allocation estimate or by an unrelated idle process. If integration places work in another process, bind that actual process and its communication cost explicitly before source freeze; do not claim a32MiB helper pass without a matching measurement.

Before generating a measured candidate row, require measured baseline B and prospective C counts, applied-template/message digests and admission decision to equal the pinned preflight evidence. For an ineligible row, bind its actually sent baseline request to the corresponding preflight count. Any mismatch is terminal: issue no generation for that row, perform owned cleanup, retain prior consumed requests and partial evidence, and do not retry or substitute an arm. Count every actual loopback attempt, including readiness, failed responses and token-count calls. The derived350+2E inventory and414 maximum are caps, never permission to add calls.

This single-pair design does not estimate between-run reproducibility. No post-hoc statistical 'instability' exclusion or threshold is permitted. For operational adjudication, the earlier phrase 'materially unstable observations' means missing/nonfinite/nonpositive elapsed values, a non-monotonic or inconsistent recorded interval, broken receipt/order binding, or a violated frozen host/resource/deadline guard. Such evidence cannot support a pass and remains incomplete/inconclusive unless another definite gate already fails. Every otherwise valid positive observation participates in its fixed latency population regardless of variance; report AB/BA subgroups and raw spread descriptively.
